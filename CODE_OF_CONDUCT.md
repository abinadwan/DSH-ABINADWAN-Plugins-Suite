# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

DSH-ABINADWAN-Plugins-Suite is an independent, open-source community project. We aim to make participation constructive, respectful, and accessible to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

This code applies to maintainers, contributors, and participants in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and related project discussions.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and give actionable, technically grounded feedback.
- Assume good faith while remaining open to correction and differing views.
- Welcome questions and contributions from people at different experience levels.
- Respect privacy; use fictional or redacted examples rather than personal records, secrets, or identifiable session logs.
- Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the reporting guidance in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, hateful language, or targeted personal attacks.
- Threats, intimidation, sexual harassment, or persistent unwanted attention.
- Publishing private information without permission (doxxing).
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or retaliation against someone who raises a concern.
- Knowingly posting credentials, unredacted personal data, or exploit details in public discussions.

Disagreement about implementation, design, or project direction is welcome when it remains respectful and focused on the work.

## Reporting concerns

If a concern can be described safely in public, alert the maintainers in a repository issue without naming affected individuals or disclosing private information. For concerns requiring confidentiality, do **not** publish the evidence in a public issue: contact the repository owner through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/abinadwan) to arrange a private channel if one is available. For abuse occurring on GitHub, including when a maintainer is involved, use [GitHub's abuse reporting](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse).

Security vulnerabilities have a separate reporting process: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not submit secrets or vulnerability details through public issue templates.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may request edits, hide or remove inappropriate content, issue warnings, or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior, its severity, and its impact. Reports will be reviewed with care, without promising confidentiality beyond what the chosen reporting channel can provide. Retaliation for good-faith reports is not acceptable.

Maintainers are expected to follow this code themselves. Project participation does not remove any applicable GitHub Terms of Service or community policies.
